# Badugi Rules — How to Play Online Badugi Poker

> Source: <https://www.pokerstars.com/poker/games/badugi/>

## What Is Badugi Poker?

Badugi is an online poker variant that originated in Asia and is based on draw poker mechanics. As a lowball game, the lowest hand wins. The distinguishing feature is its unique hand-ranking system: the strongest hands contain no pairs and feature four low cards of different suits — known as a Badugi.

## What Are the Rules for Badugi?

### Blinds

The game uses forced bets called blinds. The player to the left of the dealer posts the small blind, and the next player posts the big blind, typically twice the small blind's size.

### The Deal

Each player receives four face-down cards. The initial betting round follows, where players can call, raise, or fold. After this round, remaining players may draw — discarding unwanted cards for replacements. A second betting round occurs, followed by another draw and betting round. This repeats once more before a final showdown if multiple players remain. The best hand wins the pot.

### Basic Strategy

The objective is creating a Badugi: a four-card low hand with different suits and no pairs. The strongest starting hand is **4-3-2-A** (with each card a different suit). Hands rank by their highest card, with aces functioning as low cards and straights ignored. For example, 9-8-4-3 of different suits (a "Nine Badugi") would lose to an opponent holding 8-7-3-2. If no player achieves a Badugi, three-card hands beat two-card hands, which beat one-card hands.

## Badugi Hand Rankings

Rankings progress from weakest to strongest.

**One-card hands** (three cards of matching suit ignored)

- 5-x-x-x
- 4-x-x-x
- A-x-x-x

**Two-card hands** (two cards ignored due to duplicates)

- Q-J-x-x
- T-9-x-x
- 7-6-x-x
- 2-A-x-x

**Three-card hands** (one card ignored)

- K-2-A-x
- Q-2-A-x
- J-T-9-x
- J-T-6-x
- J-8-6-x
- 4-2-A-x
- 3-2-A-x

**Badugi** (all four cards count)

- K-Q-J-T (worst Badugi)
- 8-7-2-A
- 8-5-4-2
- 4-3-2-A (best Badugi)

Position is strategically important, as late-acting players gain information from opponents' discard counts, revealing hand strength.

## Additional Information

### Is Badugi a Limit game?

Badugi is usually played as fixed limit, which means the amount a player can bet and raise is predetermined. Players cannot go all-in as flexibly as in No Limit games unless they have fewer chips than the limit.

### What is the best hand in Badugi?

The best hand in Badugi is **4-3-2-A** with no matching suits. Unlike games such as Texas Hold'em, the lowest hand wins. A true Badugi requires no matching suits or ranks. Victory is possible without a Badugi if opponents lack one and your hand ranks higher, or by forcing folds.
